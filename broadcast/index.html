How it works

Built by engineers who troubleshoot transport streams for a living.

We already do ETR 101 290 diagnostics and TS capture analysis for broadcasters and manufacturers. Vistigo Broadcast turns that work into something that watches around the clock.

  1. Collect from the chain

    Probes, encoders and gateways push their statistics into a buffered pipeline, so a busy moment never drops data.

  2. Keep the history

    Stats are stored long enough to answer "did this happen last Tuesday too?" - with retention sized to your channel count.

  3. Correlate

    Stream errors sit on the same timeline as link loss, Starlink obstructions and switch errors from the other pillars.

  4. Alert and report

    Priority 1 errors alert straight away. Detailed reports go to your team, or to the manufacturer when the fault is theirs.
