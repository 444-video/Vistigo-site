Vistigo Broadcast

Find stream errors before your viewers do.

Vistigo Broadcast collects alarms and statistics from your probes, IP receivers and Zixi or SRT links and lines them up on one timeline. When a CC error burst starts, you can see whether it came from the source, the encoder or the network carrying it.
