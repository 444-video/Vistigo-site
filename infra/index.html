What we monitor

The kit underneath everything else.

Vistigo Infra polls your network, servers and power the way your NOC would if it never had to sleep, and turns the readings into trends you can act on.

Switches

Every port, every uplink, read over SNMP.

  • throughput
  • errors
  • discards
  • optic Rx / Tx
  • PoE
  • link flaps

Servers

Hardware health from the management controller, not just the operating system.

  • PSU status
  • PSU redundancy
  • fan RPM
  • inlet temp
  • CPU temp
  • disk health

Power

Know how long you have if the mains goes.

  • UPS load
  • UPS runtime

How it works

Proven open tools, set up properly.

Zabbix and Prometheus are trusted everywhere because they work. What usually goes wrong is the setup - templates nobody tuned, alerts nobody reads. That's the part we do for you.

  1. Discover

    Zabbix polls switches, servers, UPS and PDU over SNMP and IPMI. Prometheus exporters cover hosts and services.

  2. Tune

    Templates and thresholds tuned to your kit, so an alert means something.

  3. Store

    VictoriaMetrics keeps the long-term history for capacity planning and post-incident review.

  4. See and act

    Grafana dashboards per room, rack or customer, and alerts to Slack with the context attached.
