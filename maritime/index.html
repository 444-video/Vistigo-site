How it works

A small collector on board. The fleet view on shore.

Nothing on the ship needs an inbound connection, and the collector keeps working through whichever link is up.

  1. Collect on board

    A lightweight collector sits behind the dish and router - on a small PC or the existing edge device - and reads Starlink and cellular stats every minute.

  2. Push securely

    Readings travel over HTTPS with per-vessel credentials, tagged with the vessel and site name.

  3. Store the history

    VictoriaMetrics keeps months of history, so you can compare a route this week against the same route last month.

  4. See the fleet

    Grafana dashboards filter by vessel, site or link type, with a map and alerting built in.
