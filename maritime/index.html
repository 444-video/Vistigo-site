Where the money goes, and how you keep it

Starlink and marina internet are flat-rate. Cellular is charged by the gigabyte, and that's where a bill runs away: a dish blocked by the mast, a week in port on 5G, the crew streaming on the SIM. Vistigo watches each of those as it happens, so you can act the same day.

01

Only the SIM is costed

Vistigo knows which link each network is routed over. Traffic on Starlink or shore internet counts as zero. Only what really went over cellular is priced, at your rate per GB.

Crew Wi-Fi: one switch, two wins

Each network on board runs on its own VLAN, so Vistigo can split usage and cost between owner, guests and crew. When the crew network is switched off, its bandwidth goes back to the guests and its streaming stops costing you cellular data. The switch is logged on every graph, so you can see exactly what changed.

  • From the bridge. One button, and the bridge network itself is never switched.
  • On a schedule. Off for guest trips, back on when the yacht is in port.
  • Route-aware cost. Crew traffic is only priced while it's going over cellular.
  • Bandwidth handed back. See the crew's average speed returned to owner and guests.
  • A record of the switch. Every on and off is marked on the timeline.

Where the numbers come from

The dish only answers on the yacht's own network, so a small collector on board asks it directly. The router, modems and Wi-Fi already report to Peplink's InControl2, so we collect from there. Nothing on board accepts incoming connections.
