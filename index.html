Under the hood

Open, proven tooling - shaped by broadcast engineers.

No black box. Vistigo is built on the monitoring stack that already runs much of the internet, customised and tuned by a team that is obsessive about telemetry.

1 - Collect

Agents and exporters

Zabbix agents and SNMP, Prometheus exporters, our own Starlink collector and broadcast probe pipelines gather the data where it lives - on board, in the gallery or in the rack.

2 - Secure

Encrypted ingest

Every site pushes over HTTPS with its own credentials. Nothing needs an inbound port opening on a ship or a customer network.

3 - Store

VictoriaMetrics

A fast, compact time-series database that keeps months of history without the cost and weight of a traditional setup.

4 - See

Grafana dashboards

Customised, 444Video-branded dashboards per pillar, with alerts to Slack, email or your NOC - and a view per customer when you need one.
