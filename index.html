From the kit to your screen

No black box. It's Zabbix, Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics and Grafana, which half the internet already trusts, plus our own collectors for the equipment that only speaks its own language.

  • Read it where it lives
  • Send it out safely
  • Keep the history
  • Show it, and shout

Read it where it lives

  • On the ship: a small collector asks the Starlink dish directly
  • In the building: Zabbix agents, SNMP and iDRAC
  • In the cloud: Peplink InControl2, the SIM provider, Zixi

Send it out safely

  • Outbound only: nothing on a ship or customer network accepts connections
  • HTTPS, one login per site: checked at our proxy before it's written
  • Buffered: if the link drops, readings queue on disk and catch up

Keep the history

  • VictoriaMetrics: most numbers, for months
  • Zabbix: up/down, alarms and device logs
  • Elasticsearch: the firehose from broadcast probes

Show it, and shout

  • Grafana: a home page per customer, then one dashboard per job
  • Alerts: to Slack, email or your NOC, with the device and reading attached
  • No data is purple: a dead feed never shows green
