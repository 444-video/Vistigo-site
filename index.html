One view of everything you run.

Vessels at sea, streams on air, racks in the server room. Vistigo pulls the numbers out of the kit you already own and puts them in one set of live dashboards - so you see the problem before your customers do.

How it works

From the kit to the screen in five steps.

No black box. Vistigo is built on open, proven tooling - Zabbix, Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics and Grafana - with our own collectors filling the gaps where a device only talks its own language.

At the edge

VesselsPeplink routers, cellular modems, Starlink dishes, SIMs
Galleries and playoutProbes, receivers, encoders, Zixi and SRT gateways
Racks and roomsSwitches, servers, PDUs, UPS and batteries

Collect

On-site collectorsStarlink collector, vmagent and Telegraf, buffering to disk if the link drops
Agents and pollingZabbix agents, SNMP, iDRAC, node_exporter
Cloud API bridgesPeplink InControl2, Geniox SIMs, Zixi, TAG

Secure ingest

HTTPS, every site its own loginChecked at the proxy before anything is written
Nothing inbound on siteShips and customer networks only ever push out

Store

VictoriaMetricsFast, compact time series for months of history
ZabbixAvailability, triggers and device logs
ElasticsearchHigh-volume probe statistics and events

See and act

Grafana, 444Video-styledA card home page per customer, then a dashboard per job
Alerts that reach a personSlack, email or your NOC, with the context attached

House rule: a tile with no data turns purple, never green. A dead feed can't pass for a healthy one.
